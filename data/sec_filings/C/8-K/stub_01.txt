CITIGROUP INC. (C)

ITEM 8.01 — REGULATORY ACTION AND CONSENT ORDER

Citigroup Inc. entered into a formal agreement with the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) relating to the Company's Bank Secrecy Act / Anti-Money Laundering compliance program. Under the agreement, the Company has committed to enhance its transaction monitoring systems, increase AML staffing, and engage a third-party consultant to review its customer due diligence procedures. The Company has established a dedicated remediation team and expects to complete all required enhancements within 18 months. The agreement does not require the payment of a civil money penalty at this time.
