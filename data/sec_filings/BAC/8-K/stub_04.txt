BANK OF AMERICA CORPORATION (BAC)

ITEM 8.01 — FINRA INVESTIGATION AND SETTLEMENT

Bank of America Corporation reached a settlement with FINRA regarding supervisory deficiencies in its broker-dealer operations. The Company agreed to pay a fine of $24M and undertake remedial measures including enhanced trade surveillance, updated written supervisory procedures, and targeted training. The settlement relates to the period 2019 through 2020 and does not constitute an admission of wrongdoing by the Company.
