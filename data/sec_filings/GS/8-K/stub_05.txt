THE GOLDMAN SACHS GROUP, INC. (GS)

ITEM 8.01 — DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

The Goldman Sachs Group, Inc. became aware of a data security incident in which an unauthorized third party accessed certain customer data. The Company immediately engaged outside counsel and a cybersecurity firm, notified affected customers, and made required notifications to state attorneys general and federal regulators. Affected data may include names, account numbers, and transaction history for approximately 78440 customers. The Company has enhanced its security controls and implemented additional monitoring. This incident is not expected to have a material adverse effect on the Company's financial condition.
