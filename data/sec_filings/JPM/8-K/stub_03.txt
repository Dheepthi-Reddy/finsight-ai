JPMORGAN CHASE & CO. (JPM)

ITEM 8.01 — CHANGE IN CHIEF COMPLIANCE OFFICER

JPMorgan Chase & Co. announced that its Chief Compliance Officer (CCO) will retire effective 2022-12-31. The Company has appointed an interim CCO and commenced an external search for a permanent replacement. The retiring CCO oversaw the Company's global compliance program including AML, sanctions screening, FCPA, and regulatory affairs. The Board's Risk Committee will maintain enhanced oversight of compliance functions during the transition period.
