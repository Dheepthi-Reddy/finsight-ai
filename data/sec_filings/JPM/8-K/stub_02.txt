JPMORGAN CHASE & CO. (JPM)

ITEM 8.01 — QUARTERLY EARNINGS AND FRAUD DISCLOSURE

JPMorgan Chase & Co. today reported financial results for the quarter. The Company also disclosed that it identified a fraud incident affecting approximately 987 customer accounts. The incident involved unauthorized access through compromised credentials. The Company has notified affected customers, reset credentials, and filed required SARs with FinCEN. Estimated losses are $21M, substantially covered by the Company's fraud loss reserve. The Company has engaged a cybersecurity firm to conduct a forensic investigation.
